## The 1:1 → 1:Few → 1:Many evolution

| Stage | Year | Volume | Format mix | ARR contribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1:1** | Y1 | 12 hackathons | 100% Team Build / Embedded Sprint | £3–6M sourced |
| **1:Few** | Y2 | 8 sector cohorts + 12 1:1 | 60% 1:1, 40% sector | £8–15M |
| **1:Many** | Y3 | Lovable European Builders Flagship + 24 1:1 + 12 cohorts | All formats running | £20–40M |

The naïve assumption is that **breadth dilutes depth**. It doesn't have to — *if* the playbook is templatized.

> **1:1 generates the patterns. 1:Few stress-tests them. 1:Many distributes them.**

After ten 1:1s, the 1:Few format runs at 80% of the 1:1 quality at 30% of the marginal cost. After three 1:Few events, the 1:Many flagship produces brand at near-zero incremental Lovable cost.

## The Applications Library — the engine of scale

This is the single most important supporting investment beyond the hackathon producer role itself. **An Applications Group inside Strategic Initiatives owns the asset library** that turns each hackathon's outputs into the next hackathon's starting templates.

The library, at maturity, contains:

- **Reference Implementations** — 15–20 forkable Lovable apps for regulated verticals (insurance claims triage, underwriting, broker portal, KYC review, clinical trial intake, etc.). Every one ships with SSO, RBAC, audit logs, residency primitives.
- **Sector packs** — vertical-specific bundles (Insurance, Banking, Pharma, Retail, Public Sector). Problem-statement libraries, sample data shapes, judging rubrics, named buyer maps.
- **POC accelerators** — pre-cleared connectors (Salesforce, Workday, ServiceNow), pre-filled security questionnaires (SIG, CAIQ), DPA/DPIA templates.

**Every hackathon contributes back.** Winning prototypes get hardened and added to the library. **Each event leaves Lovable smarter.** The reference library is the moat against v0 — they ship horizontal templates; we ship vertical depth.

## The Hall of Fame — the public flywheel

A public showcase (deployed *on Lovable*, of course) of every customer hackathon's standout build, with credit to the customer team.

- **Customer benefit**: their team is publicly recognised — recruiting tool, internal pride, board-deck slide
- **Lovable benefit**: searchable proof at every prospect's first Google query
- **Compounding**: the Hall of Fame becomes the *de facto* enterprise vibe-coding case-study library
