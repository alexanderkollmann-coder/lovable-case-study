# Formats

> One offering, four formats, three audience shapes. Pick the right one and the deal closes itself.

## The format spectrum

| Format | Length | Audience | Wholesale price | When it fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Exec immersion** | Half-day | 8–12 senior leaders | £50K | Late funnel — economic buyer hasn't seen Lovable yet |
| **Team build** | Full-day | 20–40 builders | £100–150K | Core motion — converts champion-stage to MOU |
| **Sector flagship** | 2-day | 50–80, multi-account | £500K shared | Brand + flywheel — multiple customers from one vertical |
| **Embedded sprint** | 2–6 weeks | 8–12 embedded with us | £200–400K | Strategic accounts — one team, one production deliverable |

## The audience matrix — 1:1, 1:Few, 1:Many

| | **1:1 (single account)** | **1:Few (sector cohort)** | **1:Many (industry-wide)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Half-day** | Exec immersion | Vertical exec roundtable | Conference activation |
| **Full-day** | Team build *(primary)* | Sector cohort hackathon | Open community hackathon |
| **2-day flagship** | Account-wide multi-team | Joint flagship (e.g. UK Insurance Hack 2026) | Lovable's own brand event |
| **Multi-week** | Embedded sprint *(strategic)* | Sector innovation lab | n/a |

## Decision tree

1. **Has the customer signed an MSA?** — Yes: lead with Team Build. No: Exec Immersion first.
2. **Is the customer publicly comparable to peers in their sector?** — Yes: Sector Flagship as Q2 alternative. No: stay 1:1.
3. **Is the goal pipeline or brand?** — Pipeline: 1:1. Brand: 1:Many. Both: 1:Few.
4. **Strategic account with budget for a multi-week deliverable?** — Yes: Embedded Sprint. We are the vendor; they are the team.
5. **Half-day budget, exec audience already convinced?** — Skip the hackathon; they need an SoW, not a sprint.

## Why we chose Team Build (full-day, 1:1) for Allianz Partners

The decision came down to four constraints:

1. **MSA already signed** — no procurement runway needed → can execute in <30 days
2. **Three named LOB sponsors** — Claims, Underwriting, Broker Solutions — meant we needed enough builder bandwidth for *three* problem statements, not one. Team Build handles that; Exec Immersion does not.
3. **The customer wanted *prototypes*, not *slides*** — explicitly. The team-build format produces working software by 17:00.
4. **One account, one sector** — Sector Flagship would have diluted the relationship. We wanted Allianz Partners as the *anchor* reference, not as one logo of four.

The format is the message. We chose the format that *only* works if Lovable does what we say it does.

## What never works

- **More than 8 hours of build time on day 1.** Energy drops, output doesn't improve. Two 3-hour sprints with a lunch break beats one 8-hour sprint every time.
- **Open-ended "build whatever you want."** Builders freeze. Always co-design problem statements with the customer in advance.
- **More than one customer per 1:1 day.** Trying to combine two unrelated accounts always feels like neither got what they paid for.
- **Format chosen by sales without operations sign-off.** Sales optimizes for win-rate; ops optimizes for delivery quality. The wrong format costs both.

## What about pricing transparency?

Public list pricing creates inbound from the wrong tier; custom-quoted pricing slows down the right tier. The compromise: **publish ranges, not numbers**. "Starting at £50K" for Exec Immersion; "Custom quote" for Embedded Sprint.
