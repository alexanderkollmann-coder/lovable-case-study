# Value Proposition

> AI demo theatre is everywhere. Hackathons are the antidote.

## The thesis

Enterprise software buying has been overrun by **synthetic content**. Every vendor ships a polished demo video. Every deck says the same thing. Every pilot promises to deploy in 30 days. The signal-to-noise ratio for AI-native software is the worst it has ever been — and it is getting worse, not better, as generative AI floods the market with pitches no one can verify.

In that environment, **the only thing that still cuts through is direct, in-person experience.** You can fake a demo video. You cannot fake a hackathon. When 30 of an enterprise's own people walk into a room and ship working software in eight hours, the disbelief evaporates — because they *saw it happen*, in their building, on their data, with their teammates.

> **Seeing is believing. Hackathons are how we make our buyers see.**

## What this is, structurally

This is **not a marketing event**. This is a **forward-deployed pre-sales motion** — a new GTM channel for Lovable that sits in the funnel where traditional vendor demos used to live.

| Traditional pre-sales | The hackathon motion |
|---|---|
| Vendor demo in a conference room | Customer's own people building, on their data |
| 60 minutes, lossy | One day, eight working prototypes |
| One champion sees the value | 20–40 cross-functional staff become advocates |
| Conviction lives in slides | Conviction lives in working code |
| Sales cycle: 6+ months | Hackathon → MOU same-day → POC in 30d |

## Case study customer: Allianz Partners

Throughout this presentation, the case study customer is **Allianz Partners** — one of the largest insurance assistance businesses in the world (€10B+ revenue, operations across 70+ countries, deep AI mandate already on the executive agenda). They are the right anchor for three reasons:

1. **Regulated complexity** — claims, underwriting, broker portals, compliance. The kind of environment where vendors are usually told "you cannot ship in our walls."
2. **Active AI roadmap** — they are *already* spending on AI vendors. The question is not "will they buy?" — it is "from whom?"
3. **Reference flywheel** — an Allianz Partners reference unlocks AXA, Aviva, RSA, Direct Line, Hiscox by sector pattern-match. One logo becomes a sector beachhead.

The motion documented in the booths that follow has been designed against this real account.

## Why hackathons (not workshops, not demos, not trials)

- **Workshops** teach. Hackathons *prove*.
- **Demos** show what the vendor built. Hackathons show what *the customer* can build.
- **Trials** give a flat 14-day window of unsupported usage. Hackathons compress that into one day of curated magic plus a 30-day path to production.
