# Targeting

> The hackathon motion begins **before** the hackathon. Who we invite is the first decision that compounds.

## The ICP, in one sentence

**Mid-to-large enterprises with distributed product/design/engineering teams, an AI mandate already on the executive agenda, and a procurement function that hasn't yet decided between Lovable and v0.**

## How we prioritize

A three-axis score. Each axis 1–5; we run the funnel against the top quartile.

| Axis | Signal | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **NDR potential** | Active Lovable usage in pockets, request volume, internal-tool TAM | Hackathon ROI lives in expansion, not net-new |
| **Strategic narrative fit** | Public AI roadmap, regulated complexity, "innovation theatre" appetite | These accounts publicly endorse winners; reference flywheel |
| **Procurement viability** | SOC 2 already cleared, MSA signed or vendor onboarding live | Hackathon → POC stalls when security re-runs from zero |

## Verticals, ranked

1. **Insurance** — claims, underwriting, broker portals, complaints. Regulated, hungry, NDR-rich. Allianz Partners as anchor reference.
2. **Banking & FS** — KYC review, exception handling, disputes. v0 hasn't won here; door is open.
3. **Pharma & Healthcare** — clinical trial intake, adverse-event tracking. Long cycle, but flagship logos are durable.
4. **Retail & CPG** — store-ops, merchandiser tooling, loyalty. Fast to demo value, fast to expand.
5. **Professional services (Big-4 / Magic Circle)** — internal AI, knowledge-management. Hackathons are the cheapest way to walk through the front door.

## Buyer personas — who actually signs

- **Primary economic buyer**: Chief Innovation Officer / Chief Digital Officer / VP of AI Strategy
- **Hackathon champion**: Director of Digital Transformation / Head of Product Operations
- **Technical signoff**: CIO + CISO / Head of Architecture
- **Internal sponsor**: a senior LOB leader with budget — Head of Claims, Chief Underwriter, Head of Lending — depends on vertical

## What we do not target (yet)

- Pure SaaS-native scaleups — they self-serve into Lovable; hackathon is overkill
- Heavily-regulated public sector before SOC 2 Type II is fully shipped
- Accounts that already procure v0 at the platform level — competitive displacement is a different motion
