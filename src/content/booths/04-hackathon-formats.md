# Hackathon Formats

> One offering, four formats. Each format optimizes for a different stage of the customer journey.

## The 2×2 — format vs. customer goal

| | **1:1 (single account)** | **1:Few (sector cohort)** | **1:Many (industry-wide)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Half-day** | Exec immersion (~10 leaders) — pre-sales accelerator | Vertical exec roundtable + co-build | Industry conference activation |
| **Full-day** | Team build (~30 builders) — primary format | Sector cohort hackathon (4 customers × 8 builders) | Open community hackathon |
| **2-day flagship** | Account-wide multi-team competition | Joint flagship (e.g. UK Insurance Hack 2026) | Lovable's own flagship, e.g. London Builders |

## Format 1 — Exec Immersion (½-day, 1:1)

**Audience**: 8–12 senior leaders. CIO, COO, CDO, LOB heads.
**Goal**: convert a deal-stage relationship into an executive thesis on Lovable's value.
**Output**: each leader builds (or co-builds with an SE) one working internal-tool prototype against their actual problem, in 90 minutes.
**ROI**: deal velocity. Used in late-funnel cycles where the technical buyer is sold but the economic buyer hasn't seen Lovable.

## Format 2 — Team Build (full-day, 1:1) — *the primary motion*

**Audience**: 20–40 cross-functional employees. Engineers, PMs, designers, ops, marketing.
**Goal**: produce 4–8 working prototypes against pre-defined problem statements; convert top 1–2 into a 30-day paid POC.
**Output**: working apps, internal usage trial, signed option agreement, scheduled handoff to CS + SE.
**ROI**: pipeline acceleration. Hackathon → SoW in 60–90 days vs. 6+ months cold.

## Format 3 — Sector Cohort (full-day, 1:Few)

**Audience**: 4 enterprise customers from one vertical, ~8 builders each, 32 total.
**Goal**: shared problem-statement bank → cross-pollinated prototypes → competitive ranking across companies.
**Output**: vertical case-study pack, shared learning artefacts, competitive narrative ("the bank that won the UK Banking Hack…").
**ROI**: brand + flywheel. One sector cohort positions Lovable as *the* vibe-coding platform for that vertical for 12 months.

## Format 4 — Industry Flagship (2-day, 1:Many) — *Lovable's own brand event*

**Audience**: invite-only, 100–200 hand-picked builders from across the customer base + EF/YC alumni.
**Goal**: brand, talent, narrative. *Not* deal conversion (although it produces it as a side effect).
**Output**: industry coverage, a winning project Lovable can publicly back/invest in (Stardust pattern), 12 months of recruiting pull.
**ROI**: positioning. The European AI hackathon people remember three years later.

## Decision tree — which format when

- **Has the customer signed an MSA?** → Yes: Format 2. No: Format 1 first.
- **Is the customer publicly comparable to others in their sector?** → Yes: pitch Format 3 as Q2 alternative.
- **Is the goal pipeline or brand?** → Pipeline: 1 + 2. Brand: 4. Both: 3.
- **What's the budget?** → Half-day = $50K wholesale. Full-day = $100–150K. Sector cohort = $300–500K shared. Flagship = $600K–1M Lovable-funded brand spend.

## What never works

- **Open-ended "build whatever you want"** in an enterprise setting. Builders freeze. Always pre-seed problem statements co-designed with the customer.
- **More than 8 hours of build time on day 1.** Energy drops, output doesn't improve. Two 3-hour sprints with a lunch break beats one 8-hour sprint every time.
- **More than one customer per 1:1 day.** Trying to combine two unrelated accounts into one day always feels like neither got the experience they paid for.
