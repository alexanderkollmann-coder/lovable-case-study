# Value Proposition

> Two value props running in parallel. The customer's. Lovable's. They have to compound, not compete.

## For the customer — what they actually buy

Enterprises don't buy hackathons. They buy **proof that AI-native software can be built inside their walls, by their people, against their problems, in their time horizon.**

The five things they get:

1. **Working prototypes**, not slides. By end of day, two to ten working apps in their workflow, on their data shape.
2. **Internal alignment.** Twenty cross-functional people who walked into the room with different theses leave with a single shared image of what's possible.
3. **AI literacy at the leadership level.** The exec sponsor sees what their middle managers can build. That recalibrates the AI roadmap inside a single afternoon.
4. **A live, scoped POC**, with a written option agreement (Optionsvereinbarung) signed same-day. The hackathon converts to a 30-day paid POC with no procurement friction.
5. **Brand internally.** "We did the AI hackathon" becomes a board-deck slide. The CIO is now publicly committed.

## "Seeing is believing" — why this is the right frame

People are saturated with AI content. Nobody knows what really works. In that environment, *experiencing the magic* compresses years of skepticism into a single afternoon.

A hackathon is the cheapest way Lovable has to convert a $0 belief into a $1M conviction. **Vicarious participation > 100 demos.**

## For Lovable — what we get

| Outcome | How it shows up | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Pipeline acceleration** | Hackathon → 24h MOU → 30d POC → SoW. Cycle time cut by 60%+ vs. cold enterprise sale. | Hackathon-sourced ARR is the highest-velocity pipeline we have. |
| **NDR unlock** | Each hackathon plants 2-10 active Lovable users inside the account. Those seats expand 2-3x in the year that follows. | Highest single NDR lever in the company. |
| **Reference customer flywheel** | Every hackathon produces 1 publishable case study + 1 internal evangelist + 1 cross-account warm intro. | Compounds across sectors. Allianz unlocks Aviva unlocks Direct Line. |
| **Competitive displacement** | Hackathon attendees self-select Lovable over v0/Bolt for prototypes; once internal pipeline is in Lovable, switching cost compounds. | The cheapest moat we can dig. |
| **Talent flywheel** | Builders we meet become future hires, advocates, channel partners. | Same logic as YC for Garry Tan in 2010. |

## Why hackathons (not workshops, not demos)

- **Workshops** teach. Hackathons *prove*.
- **Demos** show what we built. Hackathons show what *they* can build.
- **Trials** give a flat 14-day window. Hackathons are 1 day of compressed magic plus a 30-day path to production.
