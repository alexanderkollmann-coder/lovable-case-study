# Measurement

> If we cannot tie hackathon activity to revenue, the program is brand theatre. We can. Here is how.

## North-star

**Hackathon-sourced ARR.** Every hackathon-touched account gets a tag. Net new ARR + expansion ARR from those accounts is the headline metric.

12-month target (assuming 12 1:1 hackathons in year 1):

> **£3M–6M sourced ARR + £8M–15M influenced expansion**, with hackathon attribution windowed at 12 months.

## Leading indicators — measured weekly

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **MOU same-day rate** | % of 1:1 hackathons that produce a signed Optionsvereinbarung within 24h | ≥80% |
| **POC conversion rate** | % of MOUs that activate a paid POC within 30 days | ≥65% |
| **POC-to-SoW rate** | % of POCs that become an SoW within 90 days | ≥55% |
| **Active Lovable seats per attended hackathon** | Builders still active 30 days post-event | ≥70% |
| **Hackathon NPS** | From attendees + sponsor | ≥50 |

## Lagging indicators — measured quarterly

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Hackathon-sourced ARR** | New + expansion from hackathon-tagged accounts |
| **NDR uplift** | Hackathon-attended accounts vs. matched control cohort |
| **Time-to-revenue** | Days from hackathon → first SoW invoice |
| **Reference contributions** | # of hackathon customers who agreed to be public references |

## Brand & flywheel metrics

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Press mentions** (FT, Sifted, TechCrunch, vertical trades) | Shapes EMEA narrative ahead of v0's London push |
| **Hall of Fame views + project forks** | Direct measure of post-event distribution |
| **Inbound hackathon requests** | Demand signal — when prospects ask *us* for a hackathon, the flywheel is working |
| **Recruiting pipeline contribution** | # hires sourced from hackathon attendees + judges + builders |

## What I will *not* measure

- **Builder satisfaction surveys without NPS context.** Vanity score, easily gamed.
- **Number of prototypes built.** A vanity number — quality matters, count doesn't.
- **Twitter impressions.** Not a revenue metric.

## How this compares to peer companies

- **HubSpot** measures partner-event conversion at ~30% MOU → SoW. We should beat this — Lovable's hackathon experience is more visceral.
- **Klaviyo** measured Shopify event-to-pipeline at 12-month conversion of 25%+. Hackathons should be 2–3x because the format converts faster.
- **Notion** measures community event NPS but not pipeline rigorously — we should run a tighter loop.

## How I'd report this to the exec team

A monthly hackathon dashboard, deployed *on Lovable*, with:

1. North-star ARR (sourced + influenced) with 12-month rolling
2. Funnel chart: hackathons run → MOUs → POCs → SoWs → live deployments
3. Per-account view (drill-down for any tagged account)
4. Cohort comparison: hackathon-attended vs. matched control
5. Hall of Fame engagement summary
6. Forward calendar with confidence-rated pipeline
